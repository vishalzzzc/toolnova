What is EMI?

EMI stands for Equated Monthly Instalment. It is the fixed amount generally paid every month toward a loan. An EMI usually consists of both principal and interest.

How to Use the EMI Calculator

  1. Enter the loan amount.
  2. Enter the annual interest rate.
  3. Enter the loan tenure in years.
  4. Tap Calculate EMI.

EMI Formula

EMI = P × r × (1 + r)ⁿ ÷ ((1 + r)ⁿ − 1)

Where P is the principal loan amount, r is the monthly interest rate and n is the total number of monthly payments.

Important Note

This calculator provides an estimated EMI based on the values entered. Actual loan payments may differ because of lender policies, fees, taxes, insurance, processing charges or changes in interest rates.
