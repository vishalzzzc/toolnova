What is EMI?

EMI stands for Equated Monthly Instalment. It is the fixed amount generally paid every month toward a loan. An EMI usually consists of both principal and interest.

How to Use the EMI Calculator

  1. Enter the loan amount.
  2. Enter the annual interest rate.
  3. Enter the loan tenure in years.
  4. Press Calculate EMI.

Example

For example, if the loan amount is ₹5,00,000, the annual interest rate is 8.5% and the tenure is 5 years, the calculator will show the estimated monthly EMI, total interest and total repayment.

EMI Formula

The standard EMI calculation uses the monthly interest rate, loan principal and total number of monthly payments.

Important Note

This calculator provides an estimate for informational purposes. Actual EMI, interest and repayment amounts may differ depending on the lender, fees, taxes, processing charges and loan terms.
