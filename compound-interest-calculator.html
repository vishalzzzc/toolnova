What is Compound Interest?

Compound interest means earning interest on the original investment as well as on interest accumulated during previous compounding periods.

How to Use

  1. Enter the initial investment.
  2. Enter the annual interest rate.
  3. Enter the investment period.
  4. Select the compounding frequency.
  5. Tap Calculate Interest.

Formula

A = P × (1 + r/n)ⁿᵗ

A is the final amount, P is the principal, r is the annual interest rate, n is the number of compounding periods per year, and t is the time in years.

Important Note

This calculator provides a mathematical estimate. Actual investment returns may differ because of taxes, fees, changing interest rates and investment performance.
