What is an Age Calculator?

An age calculator determines a person's age based on their date of birth and today's date. ToolNova's calculator shows the result in years, months and days.

How to Calculate Your Age

  1. Select your date of birth.
  2. Tap "Calculate My Age".
  3. Your exact age will appear instantly.

Frequently Asked Questions

How accurate is the calculator?

The calculator uses the current date on your device and calculates the difference between the selected birth date and today's date.

Can I calculate my age on mobile?

Yes. ToolNova's age calculator works on smartphones, tablets and desktop browsers.

Does ToolNova store my date of birth?

The calculation is performed directly in your browser. The page does not require an account to calculate an age.
