What is a Discount Calculator?

A discount calculator helps determine how much money is saved after applying a percentage discount to an original price.

How to Use

  1. Enter the original price.
  2. Enter the discount percentage.
  3. Tap Calculate Discount.

Example

If an item costs ₹2,000 and has a 25% discount, the discount is ₹500 and the final price is ₹1,500.

Important Note

The result is a mathematical calculation. Actual store prices may differ because of taxes, coupons, shipping charges or other offers.
