What is a Unit Converter?

A unit converter changes a measurement from one unit into another. For example, you can convert kilometers to miles, kilograms to pounds, Celsius to Fahrenheit or gigabytes to megabytes.

Available Conversions

  • Length: meter, kilometer, centimeter, millimeter, mile, yard, foot and inch.
  • Weight: kilogram, gram, milligram, pound and ounce.
  • Temperature: Celsius, Fahrenheit and Kelvin.
  • Digital data: byte, KB, MB, GB and TB.

Frequently Asked Questions

How many meters are in one kilometer?

One kilometer equals 1,000 meters.

How many pounds are in one kilogram?

One kilogram is approximately 2.20462 pounds.

How do I convert Celsius to Fahrenheit?

Multiply the Celsius temperature by 9/5 and add 32. The converter performs this calculation automatically.

How many MB are in one GB?

For digital storage calculations, ToolNova uses 1 GB = 1,024 MB.
