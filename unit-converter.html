What is a Unit Converter?

A unit converter helps convert measurements from one unit to another, such as meters to feet, kilograms to pounds and Celsius to Fahrenheit.

Supported Conversions

  • Length
  • Weight
  • Temperature
  • Data
  • Time

How to Use

  1. Select a category.
  2. Tap From and select a unit.
  3. Tap To and select a unit.
  4. Enter a number.
  5. Press Convert.
