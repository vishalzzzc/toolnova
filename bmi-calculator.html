What is BMI?

BMI (Body Mass Index) is a commonly used screening measure based on a person's weight and height. It can help categorize weight relative to height, but it is not a complete measure of individual health or body composition.

How to Use

  1. Enter weight in kilograms.
  2. Enter height in centimeters.
  3. Tap Calculate BMI.
  4. Check the calculated BMI and category.

BMI Formula

BMI = Weight (kg) ÷ Height² (m)

Important Note

BMI is a general screening measure and should not be treated as a diagnosis or a complete assessment of health.
