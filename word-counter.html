What is a Word Counter?

A word counter is a tool that counts the number of words in a piece of text. It can also count characters, sentences and paragraphs. Writers, students, bloggers and content creators often use word counters to meet writing limits.

How to Use the Word Counter

  1. Type or paste text into the text box.
  2. The word and character counts update automatically.
  3. Use Copy Text to copy your text or Clear to start again.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is this word counter free?

Does the word counter work on mobile?

Yes. The tool is designed to work on smartphones, tablets and desktop browsers.

Does it count characters with spaces?

Yes. The tool provides both total characters including spaces and characters excluding spaces.

How is reading time calculated?

Estimated reading time is calculated using approximately 200 words per minute.
