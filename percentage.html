What is a Percentage?

A percentage is a number expressed as a fraction of 100. The percentage symbol is %. Percentages are commonly used for discounts, marks, statistics, finance and everyday calculations.

How to Calculate a Percentage

To calculate a percentage of a number, multiply the number by the percentage and divide the result by 100.

For example, 20% of 500 is:

500 × 20 ÷ 100 = 100
